# Vireo Audio — Support Operations Memo

**To:** Priya Raman  
**Subject:** Support performance and Q3 training priorities  
**Period:** January 2025 – June 2026, with Q2 2026 used for the current replacement-cost baseline

## Executive summary

The support dataset contains **11,750 tickets across 44 agents**. Overall CSAT is **3.33/5**, with **5,196 survey responses**. The overall SLA breach rate is **9.1%**, and **1,896 tickets resulted in replacements**, a **16.1% replacement rate**.

The clearest measurable cost opportunity is replacement activity. In **Q2 2026**, 361 of 2,067 tickets resulted in replacements, giving a **17.46% replacement rate**. Reducing this to **15%** would correspond to approximately **51 fewer replacements per quarter** at the same ticket volume. Using the policy-defined replacement cost, this represents an estimated **₹91,263 of potential quarterly cost avoided**.

This is an opportunity estimate, not a guaranteed saving attributable to training alone.

## Training priority

The dashboard identifies **4 agents for Priority Review** and **3 for Watch**. These groups are based on CSAT performance relative to comparable peers, confidence from survey-response volume, and SLA-breach signals.

The review deliberately does **not** treat a raw bottom-ten ranking as a definitive performance ranking. Queue mix affects results, and Tier 2 work is multi-touch and measured in days. Tier 2 agents are therefore not compared with Tier 1 agents on volume metrics.

The recommended use of the **₹4 lakh Q3 training budget** is to focus coaching attention on the identified review group first, then reassess the same measures after training. The dashboard should support coaching decisions rather than automatic disciplinary or bonus decisions.

## Recommended operating focus

1. **Prioritize replacement reduction** toward a 15% rate.
2. **Review the 4 Priority Review agents** using their comparable peer context and operational signals.
3. **Monitor the 3 Watch agents** rather than treating them as confirmed performance problems.
4. Recheck CSAT, SLA breaches, handle time and replacement activity after training.
5. Keep Tier 2 performance analysis separate from Tier 1 volume comparisons.

## Measurement

The dashboard provides deterministic calculations for CSAT, handle time, SLA performance and replacement activity. A 100-ticket independent validation sample produced **0 mismatches across 500 metric comparisons (0.00% comparison error rate)**.

The replacement-cost estimate uses the support policy definition of **product unit cost + ₹340 logistics**.

**Bottom line:** the immediate measurable target is to move the Q2 replacement rate from **17.46% toward 15%**, while using the training-priority view to focus Q3 coaching without treating mixed queue or Tier 2 differences as individual performance failures.
